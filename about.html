About Antra-Web

Curiosity Started It.
Creativity Built It.

From childhood, I've always been curious about learning something new — communication, technology, design, and the endless questions that came with wanting to understand how things worked.

That curiosity meant I was constantly exploring different skills at the same time. Eventually, I realized that one of those skills could become more than something I simply enjoyed learning. It could become something meaningful — a way to build, create, and support my family.

That's when I discovered web design.
